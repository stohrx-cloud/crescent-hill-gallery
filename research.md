# Crescent Hill Gallery: research

Researched 20 November 2026. Sources: crescenthill.com (home, about, contact, exhibitions, While We Wait page, new arrivals, artists, services, framing), akimbo.ca and Visit Mississauga event listings.

## Vibe statement

Crescent Hill is a 5,600 square foot white-walled gallery on Dundas Street West that has been placing original Canadian art in homes and offices since 1985. It is knowledgeable but unpretentious: staff who will come to your house, let you take a painting home for a few days, and frame it for you. Their walls are full of colour and texture (birches, Algonquin pines, rainy Toronto streets), so the site itself should stay quiet and let the work do the talking.

## Current exhibition

**While We Wait**, Fall 2026, November 13, 2026 to January 16, 2027. Opening reception was Friday November 13, 6 to 9pm.
Curatorial statement themes: noticing the in-between days, the routes we know by heart, sun through branches, "Life does not only happen on the days we circle on the calendar."
Featured artists: Tim De Rose, Leif Ostlund, Maya Eventov, Lee Munn, Marie-Claude Boucher, Harold Braul, Pietro Adamo, Emilija Pasagic, Meredith Bingham, Victor "Nemo" Colesnicenco, Eduard Gurevich, Bruno Capolongo.

Past shows: Field of View (fall 2025), LAND + FORM (summer 2025), Resonance (spring 2025), Bijoux winter miniatures (works under 30x30 in), Gather (fall 2024), Narrative Nuance, Mark Making, CHARISMA, Ways of Seeing, Brushes with the Ethereal. Two group shows a year plus summer and winter shows.

## Selling model

- No prices online. Every artwork page has "Request Pricing" and "View On Wall", plus an ID number (e.g. 194397).
- Sizes are listed height then width in inches.
- Services: in-home trials (take a piece home for a few days), virtual installation (send a landscape photo and wall width), residential and corporate consultations including home visits, award-winning custom framing (five moulding suppliers), commissions, art rental, venue rental, local delivery and installation, worldwide shipping, restoration and conservation, working with interior designers.

## What the current site gets wrong (and the sample fixes)

1. A mailing-list pop-up covers the home page on arrival. Its consent text names a different business ("Progressive Fine Art, 2586 Dunwin Drive"). Sample: inline sign-up in the footer, no pop-up.
2. The current exhibition is not on the home page; the hero slider shows older banners. Sample: While We Wait leads.
3. Size is the hardest thing to judge online (their own in-home trial copy says so), but every thumbnail is cropped to the same square. Sample: new work is hung on one wall at true relative scale.
4. SEO-stuffed headings ("history of our toronto modern art gallery") and long paragraphs. Sample: short, plain copy in their voice.
5. Hours are only on the contact page. Sample: hours, today's status and holiday closures on the home page with a map.
