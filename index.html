While We Wait, fall 2026

Life does not only happen on the days we circle on the calendar.From the curatorial statement

Our fall exhibition, While We Wait, brings together new work by Crescent Hill Gallery's featured artists and showcases a wide range of styles and mediums.

The exhibition is about noticing what is already around us: the different textures or sheens of a surface, the sun rays through the tree branches, a particular colour or shape catching our attention.

Services

For private collectors, interior designers and businesses.

In-home trials

On white gallery walls lit to highlight the work, it is hard to judge whether a piece has the right size or texture for your space. Local clients can take a work home for a few days and see it in their own light. You may love it even more, or find it is too dark or too small for the room.

Ask about a trial

Virtual installation

Send a landscape photo of your space and the width of the wall. We will place any painting in the gallery into it, in proportion, as well as wall-hanging sculpture.

Custom framing

Award-winning framing with five moulding suppliers, from stretching and framing a canvas to fully mounted and assembled pieces.

Consultations

Residential and corporate art consulting, including visits to your home to advise on fit and placement. We also work with interior designers.

Delivery and shipping

Local delivery and installation, and specialized shipping worldwide.

Also available: commissions, art rental, restoration and conservation, and venue rental.
